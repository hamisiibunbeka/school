Chapter 1 · Start here

Getting started

Signing in, finding your way around, and the controls you will meet on every page: search, filters, date pickers, searchable dropdowns, Excel import and export.

Sign in

Open the school's web address in any modern browser (Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari) on a computer, tablet or phone. If your school's website is shown, click Portal login in its menu.

  1. 1Type the email address your school registered for you.
  2. 2Type your password. The eye icon shows what you typed.
  3. 3Tick Keep me signed in only on your own device.
  4. 4Click Sign in. You land on your dashboard (parents and students land on the portal).
Forgot your password?Click Forgot password? on the sign-in page, enter your email and click Email reset link. Open the email and choose a new password. If the email does not arrive, ask your school administrator to reset it from Administration › Users.
Two-factor sign-inIf two-factor authentication is on for your account, after your password you will be asked for the 6-digit code from your authenticator app (or one of your recovery codes).

Register a new school

A school that is not yet on the platform can sign itself up and start a free 30-day trial. Click Register your school under the sign-in button.

  1. 1Enter the school's official name.
  2. 2Choose the level (nursery, primary, secondary…) and whether it is day, boarding or both. These set sensible defaults.
  3. 3Enter your own name, phone, email and a password, then click Create school account.

Registration creates the school with a ready chart of accounts, an academic year with terms, a grading scale, departments, Tanzanian statutory payroll rules and an unpublished website. You become its School Administrator.

Find your way around

  1. 1Sidebar. Every module you have permission for. Click a module to open its pages. Modules you cannot use are not shown.
  2. 2Student search. Type a name or admission number from any page to jump to a student.
  3. 3Shortcuts to the school calendar and to Collect payment.
  4. 4School switcher. If you work for more than one school in a group, switch between them here.
  5. 5Notifications. The badge counts unread items such as approvals waiting for you.
  6. 6Theme. Cycles light, dark and your device setting.
  7. 7Your menu. Profile, password & security, and sign out.

While a page loads, a small spinner appears and the page body fades slightly. The sidebar and header stay usable.

Install as an appWhen your browser supports it, an Install app button appears in the header. Installing adds the system to your phone's home screen or your computer's start menu so it opens in its own window like a normal app.

Lists, search, filters and export

Most modules open on a list. Every list works the same way.

  1. 1Search filters as you type.
  2. 2Filters narrow the list (class, status, gender…). A Clear filters button appears when any are set.
  3. 3Export CSV downloads exactly the rows you filtered, ready for Excel. Every export is recorded in the audit trail.
  4. 4Import adds many records at once from Excel (see below).
  5. 5Row actions: view, edit and delete. Action buttons in forms (Save, Cancel) are always centred at the bottom.

Dates and date ranges

All dates are shown and typed as dd/mm/yyyy, for example 14/10/2026. Every date field has a calendar: click the field, or type the date directly.

Reports and filters use a date-range picker with ready-made periods.

  1. 1Presets: Today, Yesterday, This week, Last week, This month, Last month, This year, Last year.
  2. 2Last month means the whole previous calendar month.
  3. 3Custom range lets you click a start and an end date on the calendar.

Dropdowns you can search

Every dropdown has a search box. Click it and start typing to narrow long lists such as classes, accounts or employees.

  1. 1Type part of the name.
  2. 2Pick from the matching options.

Date of birth or age

On student, employee and admission forms you can enter either the date of birth or the age in years. Each one fills in the other.

  1. 1Date of birth, with a calendar.
  2. 2Or the age. Typing 13 sets a date of birth 13 years ago, which you can then correct.

Import from Excel

Students, guardians, employees, departments, subjects, classes, streams, library books, inventory items and suppliers can all be imported. Look for the green Import button on that list.

  1. 1Download template (.xlsx). It has the right columns and notes on each one. Fill one record per row and keep the header row.
  2. 2Drop the filled file here, or click to browse (.xlsx or .csv, up to 5,000 rows). Every row is checked first and marked valid, duplicate or invalid with the reason. Nothing is saved until you confirm.
TipFix invalid rows in Excel and upload again. Duplicates (for example a student with the same admission number) are skipped, so re-uploading a corrected file is safe.

Your profile and security

  1. Open your menu (top right) and choose Password & Security. You are asked for your password once more.
  2. Under Two-factor authentication, click enable and scan the QR code with an authenticator app (Google Authenticator, Microsoft Authenticator…).
  3. Type the 6-digit code to confirm. Save the recovery codes somewhere safe; each can be used once if you lose your phone.
RecommendedTurn on two-factor sign-in if you are an administrator, bursar, or anyone who approves payments or sees sensitive records.

Notifications

  1. 1Open takes you straight to the record: the leave request, the voucher waiting for approval, the new online application, and so on.

How an application moves

Submitted→Under review→Interview→Accepted→Enrolled

At review or interview an application can also be Waitlisted or Rejected, and the family can Withdraw at any time before enrolment. Decisions need a short note, which is kept in the history.

The applications list

  1. 1New applications sent through the school website. Click to see only those.
  2. 2The pipeline: how many applications are at each stage. Click a stage to filter.
  3. 3Online form opens the public application form, to share with parents.
  4. 4New application records a walk-in or paper application.
  5. 5Source: office or online. Online applications carry a globe badge.

Record a walk-in application

  1. Click New application.
  2. Choose the intake year, the class applied for and day or boarding.
  3. Enter the learner's details. Use the date of birth or the age.
  4. Enter the parent or guardian's name, relationship and phone, then any health notes.
  5. Click Save. The application gets a number such as APP-00041 and starts as Submitted.
  1. 1Intake year.
  2. 2Class applied for.
  3. 3Date of birth or age.

Review, interview and decide

  1. 1The buttons offer only the next allowed steps, here Start review.
  2. 2Reject or Withdraw. You are asked for a reason.
  3. 3Parent / guardian contact details and health notes.
  1. Open the application and click Start review.
  2. To invite the learner, click Schedule interview and set the date. After the interview, record the assessment score.
  3. Click Accept, Waitlist or Reject and write the decision note.
  4. For accepted learners, print the Admission letter, then click Enrol as student. Confirm the class, stream and admission date. This creates the student record, links the guardian and assigns an admission number.

Online applications

When the school website is published, parents can apply at /sites/<your-school>/admissions (see Chapter 18). Their application appears in this list with the Online badge and any uploaded documents. Staff with admissions access get a notification. Parents receive a tracking code to check progress themselves.

The exam workflow

Draft→Marks entry→Moderation→Published

Teachers can enter marks only during Marks entry. In Moderation the academic office checks results; marks can be sent back if needed. Published results appear on report cards and the parent and student portals.

  1. 1New exam: name, type (test, midterm, end of term, mock, national), term, dates, classes, out-of mark and weight.
  2. 2Enter marks.
  3. 3Results analysis.

Track mark entry

  1. 1Where the exam is in its workflow.
  2. 2The next step, here Close & send to moderation (or Back to draft).
  3. 3Completion for every class × subject. Click a percentage to open that mark sheet.

Enter marks (teachers)

  1. Open the exam and click Enter marks.
  2. Choose the class (and stream) and your subject. The learners are listed.
  3. Type each score. Tick Absent for learners who missed the paper, and add a remark if you wish.
  4. Click Save marks. You can return and continue until the exam leaves Marks entry.
  1. 1Choose the class (and stream), then your subject, to load the mark sheet.

Results analysis

  1. 1Four views: Broadsheet, Subject analysis, Grades & divisions and Top performers.
  2. 2For each learner: total, average %, grade, points and division, with class position.
  3. 3Export to CSV or print.

Report cards

Choose an exam and a class, then print. Each learner gets one page with subjects, marks, grades and teacher remarks, average, division, position, class average, the term's attendance, and spaces for the class teacher's and head's comments and signatures. Cards for unpublished exams are marked Provisional.

Grading scale

  1. 1Add or edit grade bands: score range, grade, points and remark.
  2. 2How grades, points and divisions are calculated.

Fees & Billing

Set fees per term, bill students, apply discounts, receive payments, print receipts and statements, and chase arrears. Every invoice and receipt posts to the accounting ledger automatically.

Menu
Fees & Billing › Collect PaymentPayments & ReceiptsInvoicesFee StructuresDiscounts & ScholarshipsArrears & AgingFee Categories
Who
BursarSchool adminPrincipal, auditor view

The billing cycle each term

Fee categories→Fee structure for the term→Discounts→Generate invoices→Collect payments→Follow up arrears

1 · Fee categories

  1. 1New category: tuition, boarding, meals, examination, transport and so on. Each category posts to its own income account, so the income statement shows fees by type.

2 · Fee structures

  1. 1The term you are setting fees for.
  2. 2Matrix (classes × categories) or List view.
  3. 3Copy from term copies last term's fees, optionally adjusted by a percentage.
  4. 4Add fee line: category, classes, applies to (all, day or boarding), amount and due date.

3 · Discounts and scholarships

  1. 1Grant discount to a student: sibling discount, bursary, staff child, waiver…
  2. 2A percentage or a fixed amount, for one category or all. Discounts are applied when invoices are generated.

4 · Generate invoices

  1. 1Generate for term bills every active student from the fee structure, discounts included.
  2. 2Manual invoice bills one student for extras such as uniform or a trip.
  3. 3Filter to overdue invoices.
  4. 4Collect a payment straight from an invoice row.
  1. Click Generate for term. Choose the term, issue date, due date and classes.
  2. Check the preview: active students, invoices to issue, students already invoiced (skipped), and the total to bill.
  3. Confirm. Each invoice gets a number like INV-00421 and is posted to Student Fees Receivable. Running it again never double-bills a student.

5 · Collect a payment

  1. 1Search the student by name or admission number. Their outstanding invoices load.
  1. 1The student's outstanding invoices and balances.
  2. 2Allocate manually to choose which invoices the money pays.
  3. 3The amount received. Pay full balance fills it in for you.
  4. 4The method: cash, bank, mobile money, cheque or card.
  5. 5The reference from the bank slip or mobile-money SMS. Each reference can only be used once per method, which prevents double posting.
  1. Enter the amount received. It is applied to the oldest invoices first. Tick Allocate manually to choose which invoices it pays.
  2. Choose the Method and type the Transaction reference from the bank slip or SMS.
  3. Check the Payment date, Paid by and the Deposit account (cash, CRDB, NMB, M-Pesa float…).
  4. Click Save & issue receipt. The official receipt opens, ready to print. The money is posted to the chosen account and the student's balance drops at once.

Receipts

  1. 1Print receipt (it can be reprinted any time).
  2. 2Void a receipt entered in error. A reason is required and the ledger entry is reversed. Receipts are never deleted.
  3. 3The student's full statement.
  4. 4The invoices this payment cleared.
  1. 1Receive a new payment.
  2. 2Collections by month.
  3. 3Collections by method: cash, bank, mobile money.

Statements and arrears

  1. 1Statement period, or full history.
  2. 2Print statement for the parent: every invoice and receipt with a running balance.
  3. 3Take a payment from here.
  1. 1Aging buckets: how long balances have been owed (current, 1–30, 31–60, 61–90, 90+ days).
  2. 2Outstanding balances by class.
  3. 3Send reminders by SMS to the primary guardian of each student in arrears. The date of the last reminder is shown per student.

Menu

Who
BursarSchool adminPrincipal, auditor view
New to accounting?Read the Accounts primer first. It explains debits and credits, every field on the account form, sub-types, red balances, and exactly what each module posts. It includes worked examples and a self-check for training sessions.
Maker and checkerManual journals and payment vouchers need two people: one prepares, a different user approves. You cannot approve your own entry. Posted entries are never edited or deleted. To correct one, you reverse it, so the audit trail stays complete.

Chart of accounts

  1. 1Live balances for assets, liabilities, equity & funds, income and expenses, from posted journals. Click one to filter.
  2. 2New account with its code, class and sub-type.
  3. 3Add sub-account under a group. Accounts in use can be deactivated but not deleted.

Manual journals

Draft→Submitted (awaiting approval)→Posted→Reversed (only if a correction is needed)
  1. 1Journals waiting for a checker. It also tells you how many you can approve.
  2. 2New journal entry.
  1. 1Date, journal type, reference and a narration explaining the entry.
  2. 2One line per account, with a debit or a credit.
  3. 3Add line as needed. The = button fills the amount that balances the entry.
  4. 4Save as draft to finish later, or Submit for approval. Submitting needs total debits equal to total credits.
  1. 1The checker clicks Approve & post. The ledger updates at once.
  2. 2Or Reject it back to the preparer with a reason.
  3. 3The audit trail: who prepared, submitted and approved, and when.

Expenses and payment vouchers

Pending→Approved→Paid·Rejected / Void
  1. 1New voucher: payee, expense account, amount, payment method and supporting details.
  2. 2Approve & pay (by a different user) posts the expense and the payment.
  3. 3Vouchers waiting for approval, with their total.

Ledger and financial statements

Every report has a period or an as at date, an optional comparison, and CSV and Print buttons. Click any account line to drill down to its ledger.

Levies and compliance

Government levies and licences that are not part of payroll: service levy, fire, OSHA, property tax, and any others you add.

  1. 1Add obligation: name, authority, how it is calculated (fixed or a rate on a base), how often and when it is due.
  2. 2Record payment when paid. The payment history is kept for inspections.

Budgets

  1. 1Edit budget: annual amounts per income and expense account. The page compares budget with actual and shows the variance.

Fiscal years

  1. 1Close year after the audit. Nothing can be posted into a closed year.
  2. 2New fiscal year to open the next one.

Chapter 11 · People & operations

Payroll

Monthly payroll with Tanzanian statutory deductions (PAYE, NSSF, WCF, SDL, HESLB) calculated for you, approval by a second person, posting to the ledger, payslips and a bank file. You can pay some staff now and hold others with a reason.

Menu
HR & Payroll › Payroll RunsSalary ComponentsStatutory Rules (PAYE, NSSF…)
Who
HR / payroll officerBursarSchool adminAuditor views

The monthly run

Draft→Approved & posted→Partially paid→Paid
  1. 1New payroll run for a month. Payslips are calculated for every active employee.
  2. 2Salary components: allowances and deductions.
  3. 3Payroll cost this year.
  1. 1Progress: prepared → approved & posted → paid, with who did each step and when.
  2. 2The person who prepared the run cannot approve it. Another user with payroll rights must.
  3. 3Totals for gross pay, deductions, net pay and employer cost. Lower down: the statutory summary and the ledger entries that post on approval.
  1. 1Recalculate after changing salaries, components or statutory rules (draft runs only).
  2. 2Bank file for the bank's bulk-payment upload; Summary for the printable payroll summary.
  3. 3Open any employee's payslip.
  1. Check the draft: payslips, the statutory summary and the ledger entries.
  2. A different user clicks Approve & post. Salaries, deductions and employer contributions are posted to the ledger as liabilities.
  3. Click Pay salaries. Every employee is ticked. Untick anyone you are not paying now and pick or type the reason (for example "Bank details missing / incorrect", "Disciplinary hold" or "On unpaid leave"). Staff without bank details are flagged No bank.
  4. Choose the account the salaries are Paid from and the payment date, then click Pay selected. The run becomes Partially paid if anyone was held back.
  5. Later, open the run and click Pay remaining to pay the Previously withheld staff. Bank file (unpaid) downloads a bank file for just those staff. When everyone is paid the run is Paid.

Salary components

  1. 1New component: an allowance (housing, transport, responsibility…), a deduction (loan, SACCOS, advance…) or an employer contribution. A fixed amount or a percentage of basic salary, posted to the GL account you choose, and optionally applied to all staff.
  2. 2Assign to employees, with an amount per person if it differs.
  3. 3Employee assignments shows who receives what.

Statutory rules: PAYE, NSSF, WCF, SDL, HESLB

The rules are shown openly so every staff member can see how their deductions are worked out. Payroll administrators can edit them when the government changes rates.

  1. 1Tabs: the PAYE table, Contributions & deductions, each employee's Staff statutory profile, and official Sources.
  2. 2Edit table changes the PAYE bands. New version keeps the old table for past months and starts the new one from a date you choose.
  3. 3PAYE calculator: enter any gross pay to see the tax band by band.
Check before each runConfirm the PAYE bands and contribution rates match the latest Tanzania Revenue Authority, NSSF and WCF guidance. Changes apply to runs calculated or recalculated after you save.
